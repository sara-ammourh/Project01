Why I Am Learning to Code


Motivation

"Where is your motivation?"

Learning is one of the very important things to me. When I learn new things and practice them, it makes me proud and want to keep going on. Coding in particular is very fun to do, and in a way, it enables me to work with my creativity.


Ambition

Knowledge is one of the greatest things to pursue. It is not just about simply coding, it is about connecting with the computer, creating communities, sharing learned experiences, and achieving wonders.

My goal is to contribute to our communities by developing accessible solutions and discovering the beauty of programming.
